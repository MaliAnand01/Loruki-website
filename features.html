The Loruki Platform

Loruki provides an intuitive cloud management platform that makes deploying, scaling, and managing web applications fast and easy. With high availability, automated backups, and seamless integrations, our platform empowers developers and businesses to focus on building, not infrastructure.

Reliable and fast cloud servers optimized for performance and uptime, ensuring your applications are always accessible.

Advanced network configurations with built-in load balancing, firewalls, and secure connections for robust app delivery.

Developer-friendly tools with full CLI support, API access, and Git-based deployment workflows for agile development.

Managed databases with automated backups, scaling, and easy integration to keep your data safe and accessible.

Instant provisioning and one-click power management to start, stop, or reboot your services anytime with ease.

Seamless code uploads and deployment pipelines ensure your updates go live without hassle or downtime.
